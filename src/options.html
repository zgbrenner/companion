How the connection works

The active organization is discovered from Claude's own lastActiveOrg cookie, falling back to /api/organizations. The monthly cap comes straight from Claude's usage response (/usage or overage_spend_limit) — nothing is hardcoded.

Only the non-secret organization identifier is cached locally (up to 48 hours) so limits load instantly. The extension never reads, stores, or transmits your session cookie, and makes only read requests — it can't change your account.

Data & privacy

Everything stays on this device.

What's stored
  • Stored locally: your settings, and daily spend totals (dollar amounts and derived token ranges).
  • Never stored: the text of your prompts or Claude's replies, file contents, or your login — none of it is saved, and nothing is ever sent to any server but Claude's own.
Export usage history
One CSV row per day, week, or month: real dollars plus an approximate token range. Handy for expense notes. No chat text is included.
Clear all local data
Removes settings, spend history, and caches. Your Claude account is not touched.
