How account detection works

Claude Companion checks Claude.ai's active-organization identifier first, then asks Claude for the list of organizations available to the signed-in account. It uses read-only usage requests and never stores the authentication cookie.

Export usage history

Download one CSV row per day with dollar totals and approximate token ranges. Chat text is never included.

What the numbers mean

Dollar amounts come from Claude's usage-credit counter. Session and daily totals reflect account activity observed during those time windows, including other Claude tabs or devices. Token figures are estimates derived from the dollar amount and model pricing.

Clear Claude Companion data

Remove settings, usage history, session data, detected account details, update state, and the saved update-folder permission. Your Claude account and conversations are not affected.
